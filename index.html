Field manual

How to play

The mission

Every army starts in its own corner of a map covered in fog. Find the enemy, shoot first, keep your general alive, and destroy every enemy base. Lose your own base and it's over.

Your army

Your soldiers come in squads A, B and C, plus one general (the star). Fallen soldiers respawn at your base after a few seconds, and the general takes longer. Out of combat, everyone slowly patches up.

The general

He sees and shoots much further than infantry, is a little faster, and has ten times the health. Soldiers near him hit 30% harder. Use him for sneaky long-range shots, and don't lose him.

Moving around

  • Drag anywhere to move the map.
  • Pinch (or mouse wheel) to zoom.
  • Tap the minimap to jump anywhere.
  • BASE jumps home.
  • Tap a squad button twice to find that squad.

Giving orders

  • Tap a squad button (A B C D), GEN or ALL, or tap one of your soldiers to pick their squad.
  • ALL picks everyone, general included. ARMY picks every soldier but leaves the general where he is.
  • Then tap where they should go. Tap an enemy to attack it.
  • With Basic controls, soldiers stop to fight any enemy their group spots, then carry on to where you sent them. With Expert controls you choose: RUN & GUN keeps going and shoots whatever is in reach on the way; ENGAGE stops, and when anyone in the group spots an enemy the whole group closes in and fights until it's gone.
  • Tap the same spot twice to break off: they go straight there and only shoot on the way.
  • Press and hold the map: let go to make your soldiers run there without stopping to fight (a few seconds, then they need to get their breath back), or drag to box-select. Hold on an enemy to make everyone you picked shoot at it, for example their general.

Press and hold

  • Hold, then let go on open ground: the selected soldiers sprint there and ignore enemies on the way. Running uses the yellow stamina bar; when it runs out they walk until it refills.
  • Hold, then drag: draw a box to select everyone inside it.
  • Hold on an enemy: focus fire, so every selected soldier shoots that one target.
  • Tap the same spot twice, quickly: break off a fight and go straight there, only shooting on the way.

Basic and Expert controls

The game starts with Basic controls: squads, GEN, ARMY, ALL, BASE, STRIKE and KILL. That is all you need to win. Soldiers fight any enemy they spot and spread out on their own when plague hits, and missions join after your first 3 battles.

Turn on Expert in Settings for formations, drawn routes, rally points, guard duty, merging squads, SELECT, IDLE and the order modes. Sections marked Expert below need it.

Picking exactly who you want Expert

  • Tap SELECT, then drag a box or tap soldiers one by one.
  • Hold A, B, C or D to save the selection as that group.
  • IDLE picks everyone standing around.

Supply crates

Crates parachute onto the map every minute or so, and everyone can see where they land. Walk any soldier over one to open it. Most are good (an army upgrade, mercenaries for 60 seconds, healing, rapid fire, sharpshooters with longer range, scouts that lift the fog, camouflage, or a strike you aim with STRIKE: a boulder, a cannonball or an airstrike, depending on your era). Some are bad (plague that spreads between soldiers standing close, a trap, lost orders, a signal fire that shows your squad to the enemy, or deserters). Recruits ignore crates. Tougher enemies hunt them.

Missions

Every couple of minutes new orders bring a side mission with a countdown, shown under the scores and as a gold marker on the map and minimap. Pull it off and your army gets an upgrade, mercenaries for 60 seconds and a round of healing.

  • Supplies: grab the ★ crate before the enemy does.
  • Escort: the supply cart (a sled, wagon, truck or drone, by era) moves home only while your soldiers walk next to it.
  • Hold the hill: keep soldiers inside the circle, with no enemies in it, for 30 seconds.
  • Rescue: find the missing scout in the marked area, then walk him home.
  • Assassinate: an enemy captain with bodyguards is in the marked area. Kill him.
  • Sabotage: destroy the supply camp near an enemy base.

Recruits leave missions alone. Veterans fight over the hill and the cargo. Elite and Invincible fight you for every mission.

In Settings you can turn crates and missions on or off, and pick which mission types you want.

Squads Expert

  • Each squad button shows its health and a dot for what it's doing: grey idle, gold moving, orange fighting, blinking red under fire.
  • Tap two buttons quickly (A then B, or a squad then GEN) to command them together.
  • Drag from a squad button onto the map to draw a route for it to follow. Drag it onto another letter to merge the squads.
  • Long-press your selected soldiers, then drag them onto a letter to move them into that squad.
  • FORM switches the formation. Box is plain. Line holds the line: 15% better aim while standing still. Wedge is the charge: 10% faster, and 25% harder hits for the first 3 seconds of a fight. Each squad remembers its own.
  • REGROUP pulls a scattered squad together where most of it stands. GUARD puts the selected soldiers in a ring round the general. They follow him and take 15% less damage while they stay close.
  • RALLY sets where fallen soldiers gather when they come back. Without one, they walk back to their own squad.
  • Pick how many squads you start with in Settings. During a battle, tap ⋯ to show every order and ▾ to fold them away.
  • Mercenaries wear a gold ring, and the arc around them shows how long they stay. They join the squad that found them.

Spread out Expert

With Basic controls this happens on its own when plague hits. In Expert, tap SPREAD to open up the selected soldiers with wide gaps. Do it against plague, strikes and traps. The general always gets right of way through his own soldiers.

Time lapse

Tap the 1× button by the clock to change game speed, from ¼× slow motion up to 8× fast forward. Fast forward is fair game. If you use slow motion, that game's score stays on your device and doesn't go on the public leaderboard.

Kill selected

Trapped and outnumbered? Tap KILL, then pick who goes: the selected soldiers, the ones on screen, all but the general, the general on his own, or everyone. Nobody gets points, and they respawn at base. Better than handing the enemy free kills. The AI does it too.

Points and upgrades

Every kill is a point. Each upgrade adds 15% health and firepower to your whole army. Game pace sets how often upgrades come: every 25 (Quick), 50 (Medium) or 100 (Slow) points.

Level 9: the mystery weapon

  • The first army to reach L9 gets a mystery weapon. It charges for 20 seconds while that army's general is alive, and then that army wins.
  • Everyone sees the countdown and where the general is. Kill him and the charge resets, and you get mercenaries and healers. It starts again when he is back from base. If he is already down when L9 is reached, it waits for him.
  • What the weapon is stays a surprise until it fires.

Eras

  • Every army starts in the Stone Age and moves up one age with each level: L1 Stone Age (slings), L2 Bronze Age (javelins, a chariot), L3 Middle Ages (bows, a castle, a horseman), L4 Musket Age (muskets, a star fort), L5 World Wars (rifles, a jeep, trenches), L6 Modern Age (assault rifles, a tank), L7 Future (lasers, a hover bike) and L8 Space Age (plasma, a mech walker).
  • The era is the look of each army's upgrades: stones, javelins, arrows and plasma fly for a moment before they land, bullets and lasers hit at once. So an enemy in a later age is an army several upgrades ahead of you.

Score and leaderboard

Score = (kills × 5, plus 1,000 for a win and up to 900 for speed, and a loss keeps only a quarter) × skill (Recruit 1, Veteran 2, Elite 3, Invincible 5) × odds (enemy soldiers ÷ yours). Beat bigger, tougher armies with fewer soldiers to climb the board. Invincible can't be beaten at even odds, so shrink their armies and work your way up.

Marks on your soldiers

  • Pale ring: selected.
  • Bar underneath: health, shown once a soldier is hurt.
  • Gold ring and arc: mercenary. The arc shows how long they stay.
  • Green bubbles and dashed ring: plague. It jumps to soldiers close by, so SPREAD out.
  • Dot above the head: a crate effect. Orange rapid fire, blue sharpshooters, grey orders lost, blinking red signal fire (the enemy can see you).
  • Your army level is the L number at the top left.

Keyboard and mouse

Mouse works like touch. Optional shortcuts: [ and ] to slow down or speed up time, \ for normal speed, shift-drag to box-select, right-click to order, 1 to 4 for squads, G general, Q all, Z army (all but the general), I idle, Ctrl+number to save a group, E to switch mode, X twice to kill selected, H for base, P or Esc to pause.
